# Inventory Validation Analysis

## Module Overview

This module analyses inventory validation records to evaluate validation quality, warehouse performance and operational compliance.

---

## Business Objectives

- Analyse validation workload
- Identify failed validation records
- Compare warehouse validation performance
- Measure validation success rate
- Detect operational risks

---

## Tables Used

- inventory_item_master
- warehouses

- COUNT

---

## Business Value

Validation analysis improves inventory accuracy by identifying failed validation processes and helping operations teams strengthen warehouse controls.
