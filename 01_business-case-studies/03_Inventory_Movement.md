# Inventory Movement Analytics

## Module Overview

This module analyses inventory movement transactions to understand warehouse activity, item movement patterns and operational workload.

The objective is to identify high-movement warehouses, frequently moved items and inventory flow patterns.

---

## Business Objectives

This module answers business questions such as:

- How many inventory movements happen across warehouses?
- Which warehouses handle the highest movement volume?
- Which items are moved most frequently?
- Which inventory items have the highest movement quantity?
- What is the average transaction movement size?

## Business Value

Inventory movement analytics helps operations teams understand warehouse workload, identify fast-moving products and improve inventory planning.

Insights from movement analysis can support:

- Warehouse resource planning
- Inventory monitoring
- Operational efficiency improvement
- Better stock management
