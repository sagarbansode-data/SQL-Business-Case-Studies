# Inventory Analytics

## Module Overview

This module focuses on analysing inventory items, suppliers and product categories using SQL.

The objective is to generate business insights related to inventory management, pricing and supplier contribution.

---

## Business Objectives

This module answers questions such as:

- How many inventory items are available?
- Which inventory items are the most expensive?
- Which suppliers provide the highest number of products?
- What is the average price by category?
- How are inventory items distributed across categories?

---

## Tables Used

- inventory_item_master
- suppliers
- item_categories

---

## SQL Concepts Covered

- SELECT
- JOIN
- GROUP BY
- COUNT
- AVG
- ORDER BY
- Subqueries

---

## Business Value

Inventory analytics helps organisations understand product distribution, supplier relationships and pricing trends to support inventory planning and procurement decisions.
