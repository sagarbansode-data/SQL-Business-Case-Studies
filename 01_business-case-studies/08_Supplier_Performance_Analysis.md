# Supplier Performance Analysis

## Module Overview

This module analyses supplier performance using inventory master data.

The analysis focuses on supplier contribution, product portfolio, pricing trends and category distribution.

---

## Business Objectives

- Analyse supplier contribution
- Compare supplier pricing
- Rank suppliers
- Evaluate category distribution
- Generate executive supplier reports

---

## Tables Used

- suppliers
- inventory_item_master
- item_categories

---

## SQL Concepts Covered

- JOIN
- GROUP BY
- Aggregate Functions
- RANK()
- CTE
- ORDER BY
- LIMIT

---

## Business Value

Supplier analysis helps procurement teams identify strategic suppliers, evaluate pricing trends and optimise sourcing decisions.
