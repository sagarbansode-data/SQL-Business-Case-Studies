# Staff Productivity Analysis

## Module Overview

This module analyses warehouse staff performance, workload distribution and task allocation.

The objective is to identify highly productive employees, workload imbalance and operational efficiency.

---

## Business Objectives

- Analyse employee workload
- Measure staff productivity
- Rank employees based on completed tasks
- Identify top performers
- Detect workload imbalance

---

## Tables Used

- warehouse_staff
- task_allocation_log
- warehouses

---

## SQL Concepts Covered

- JOIN
- GROUP BY
- Aggregate Functions
- CTE
- RANK()
- DENSE_RANK()
- ROW_NUMBER()

---

## Business Value

Staff productivity analysis supports workforce planning, task balancing and warehouse operational efficiency.
