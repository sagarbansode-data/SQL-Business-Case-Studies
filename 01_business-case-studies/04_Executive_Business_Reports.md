# Executive Business Reports

## Module Overview

This module contains executive-level SQL reports designed for business managers and stakeholders.

Unlike basic SQL queries, these reports combine multiple business tables to generate insights that support operational and strategic decision-making.

---

## Business Objectives

This module answers questions such as:

- Which warehouses are performing the best?
- Which suppliers contribute the most inventory items?
- How are products distributed across categories?
- Which staff members handle the highest workload?
- What are the key operational KPIs?

---

## Tables Used

- warehouses
- warehouse_staff
- inventory_item_master
- inventory_movement_log
- inventory_validation_log
- task_allocation_log
- suppliers
- item_categories

---

## SQL Concepts Covered

- INNER JOIN
- LEFT JOIN
- Aggregate Functions
- GROUP BY
- ORDER BY
- Subqueries
- Business KPI Reporting

---

## Business Value

These reports simulate stakeholder requests commonly received by Data Analysts.

The outputs support warehouse management, supplier evaluation, inventory planning and executive decision-making.
