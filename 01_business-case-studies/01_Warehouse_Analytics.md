# Warehouse Analytics

## Module Overview

This module contains SQL queries developed to analyse warehouse information and operational performance.

The objective is to understand the warehouse network, storage capacity and geographical distribution to support operational decision-making.

---

## Business Objectives

The SQL queries in this module answer the following business questions:

- What warehouses are available in the organisation?
- How many warehouses are operational?
- Which states contain the highest number of warehouses?
- Which warehouses have the highest storage capacity?
- How is warehouse information organised for reporting purposes?

---

## Tables Used

- warehouses

---

## SQL Concepts Covered

- SELECT
- ORDER BY
- COUNT
- GROUP BY
- Aggregate Functions

---

## Business Value

Warehouse analytics helps operations teams understand the warehouse network and supports planning, capacity management and operational reporting.
